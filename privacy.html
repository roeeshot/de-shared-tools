DE Shared Tools privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026.

Purpose and information accessed

DE Shared Tools supports private Google Drive automation for accounts authorized by its operator. Depending on the permissions granted, installed tools can access account identity, file names and metadata, and file contents, and can upload, update or delete files. Access is used to perform the operator's requested file-management and encrypted-backup workflows.

Storage and protection

Authorization tokens and application credentials are kept in access-restricted configuration on operator-controlled systems. Backup content is encrypted before storage in Google Drive. Restored or working copies may temporarily exist on operator-controlled systems for processing or recovery verification. Credentials, backup content and private account information are not included in this public website or repository.

Use and sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models by this application. Data is processed by the operator and the services required for the authorized workflows, including Google Drive for cloud storage. This application does not provide public access to private files.

Retention and control

Files and backups are retained according to the operator's configured retention and recovery requirements. You can revoke the application's access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase existing backups or working copies. The operator manages their deletion and retention.

This website and contact

These information pages use no application analytics, advertising or sign-in forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

For questions or deletion requests, contact the operator. GitHub issues are public: do not include private files, passwords, tokens or account details. Request a private contact channel for sensitive matters.
